0xSubRosa is an independent research group working on zero knowledge proofs, post-quantum cryptography, the formal verification of cryptographic systems, and distributed systems. We write papers, release open-source code, and work with protocols to take these ideas from research into production.

We believe privacy is a human right, and that it should be normal on the internet. We take on open problems in zero knowledge and cryptography, and we build production systems that make private use the default. 0xSubRosa takes funding only from grants and donations.
